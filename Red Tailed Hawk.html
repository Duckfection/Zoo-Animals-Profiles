Red Tailed Hawk

Scientific Classification

  • Conservation status: Least Concern
  • Kingdom: Animalia (Animals)
  • Phylum: Chordata (Chordates)
  • Class: Aves (Birds)
  • Order: Accipitriformes (Hawks, Eagles, Kites)
  • Family: Accipitridae (Hawks, Eagles, Kites)
  • Genus: Buteo
  • Species: Buteo jamaicensis

Physical Profile

Male

  • Weight: 24-46 oz
  • Height: 11-16 inches
  • Length: 17-22 inches
  • Wingspan: 45-52 inches
  • Lifespan: Up to 20 years

Female

  • Weight: 32-52 oz
  • Height: 11-16 inches
  • Length: 19-26 inches
  • Wingspan: 45-52 inches
  • Lifespan: Up to 20 years

Habitats

  • They can be found in a variety of habitats, across the US, Southern Canada and Central America.

Ecology & Behavior

  • Diet: They eat small mammals, birds, reptiles. Diet can differ between regions, seasons, and individuals.
  • Behaviors & Adaptations: They hunt during the day. They have excellent eyesight and can spot prey from great distances. They'll adapt their diet to whatever is available.
