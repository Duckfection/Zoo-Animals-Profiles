Emerald Tree Boa

Scientific Classification

  • Conservation status:Least Concern
  • Kingdom:Animalia
  • Phylum:Chordata
  • Class:Reptilia
  • Order:Squamata
  • Family:Boidae
  • Genus:Corallus
  • Species:Corallus caninus

Physical Profile

  • Weight:1.5-3.5 lb
  • Body Length:4-6 ft
  • Lifespan:15-20 years

Habitat

  • Tropical Rainforests

Ecology & Behavior

  • Diet: Small mammals, birds, lizards, and frogs
  • Behaviors & Adaptations: Emerald Tree Boas are nocturnal ambush predators with excellent camouflage
  • Communication: Emerald tree boas are solitary animals that interact primarily with a mate and there prey. Occasionally, aggressive behavior occurs when males get close to one another.
  • Life History Cycle: AFter birth Emerald tree boas have different colors like red, yellow, or orange and it takes around a year to get that signature green color. The babies are able to hunt for food right after they are born. They usually grow to maturity in about 3-4 years for males and 4-5 years for females.
  • Breeding Behaviors:Emerald Tree Boa's breeding season is through April-July. They reproduce once every 2 years and do not lay eggs. They have a gestation period of 6-7 months.
  • Predators: The preditors of the Emerald Tree Boa include large birds of prey, primarily the Guinean crested eagles.
