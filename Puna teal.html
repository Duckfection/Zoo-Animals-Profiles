Puna Teal

Scientific Classification

  • Conservation status:Least Concern
  • Kingdom:Animalia (Animals)
  • Phylum:Chordata
  • Class:Aves
  • Order: Anseriformes
  • Family: Anatidae
  • Genus: Spatula
  • Species:Spatula puna

Physical Profile

  • Weight:1-1.5 lbs
  • Body Length:19 inches
  • Wingspan: 29-33 inches
  • Lifespan:10-15 years

Habitats

  • Inland wetlands

Ecology & Behavior

  • Diet:Seeds, small fish, snails, and crustaceans
  • Behaviors & Adaptations: They live in small groups of ther own kind or with the closely related Silver Teal. They largly stay in the same area year round (high altatude wet lands).
  • Communication:They are generally quiet birds, communicating through subtle body language and soft calls.
  • Life History Cycle:Once hatched, the ducklings are precocial (They are covered in down makeing them able to leave the nest).Then they re guided and protected by the female untill the fledge 50-55 days later.
  • Breeding Behaviors: There breeding season is through october-March, when the conditions are favorable. They usualy nest near edges of lakes and marshes. Incubation period is carried out by the female that last 24-25 days.
  • Predators: There predators include birds of prey amd terrestrial mammals.
