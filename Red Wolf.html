Red Wolf

Scientific Classification

  • Conservation status: Critically endangered
  • Kingdom: Animalia (Animal)
  • Phylum: Chordata (choradates)
  • Class: Mammalia (Mammals)
  • Order: Carnivora (Carnivores)
  • Suborder: Caniformia
  • Family: Canidae
  • Genus: Canis
  • Species: rufus

Physical Profile

  • Weight: 45-80lb
  • Height: 26-31 inches
  • Body Length: 39-51 inches
  • Tail Length: 12-17 inches
  • Lifespan: 6-8 years in the wild and up to 15 years in captivity

Habitats

  • Forests
  • Grasslands
  • Swamps

Ecology & Behavior

  • Diet: White-Tailed Deer, Raccoons, turkeys, oppssums, nutria, and smaller animals(rabbits,birds, and Mice).
  • Behaviors & Adaptations: Primaraly Nocturnal animals. There packs normaly consist of the mated pair and their pups, but larger packs have been reported. Packs normaly live peacfully along side eachother, though aggression to unkown wolfs is a characteristic. They hunt over a period of 7-10 days, shifting locations in the territory.
  • Communication: They communicate via barks, growls, yaps, howls, and body language.
  • Life History Cycle: They are born in a litter of 3-6 pups. Both parents care for the young, btinging food back untill they are old enough to join the hunt. They grow to fullsize and sexually mature in around a 2-3 years.
  • Breeding Behaviors: There breeding season from january-March.There gestation period is from 60-63 days, with the litter being 3-6 pups.
  • Preditors: They are nnormaly killed due to altercations with gray wolves and coyotes. Young wolves can also be preyed on by large predators like alligators, large raptors, and bobcats.
